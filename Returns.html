Returns & Refund Policy

At Al Yaser we care about your complete satisfaction with your purchases. Please read our returns and refund policy carefully before placing any order.

1. Return Window

  • You may request a return within 14 days of receiving your order.
  • The period starts from the date of delivery or the first delivery attempt.

2. Return Conditions

  • Items must be unused, unworn, and in original packaging.
  • All tags and labels must remain intact and attached.
  • Altered or custom-made items are not eligible for return.

3. Return Options

  • You may exchange the item for another size or colour, subject to availability.
  • You may receive a full refund to the original payment method.
  • You may receive store credit for use on your next purchase.

4. How to Return

  • Contact our customer service to obtain a return authorization number.
  • Ship the item in good condition with the receipt or order number attached.
  • The item will be inspected before the return or refund is approved.

5. Refunds

  • Once a return is approved, refunds are processed within 5-7 business days.
  • The refunded amount will appear in your account depending on the original payment method and bank processing time.
  • Shipping fees are not refunded except in cases of incorrect shipment.

6. Non-Returnable Items

  • We do not accept returns of items damaged by misuse over time.
  • Some items such as final sale or special items are excluded.

7. Contact Us

  • For any questions regarding returns or refunds, contact us via the Contact Us page or through the phone numbers listed on our branches.
